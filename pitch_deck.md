# SLIDE 02 — THE PROBLEM

# Banking Products Are Everywhere. Relevant Recommendations Aren't.

Banks offer customers numerous financial products, but traditional marketing often fails to reflect individual financial needs, preferences and timing.

### The Challenges

**Poor Personalization**

Customers with different financial needs often receive similar product promotions.

**Wrong Timing**

Products may be recommended without considering the customer's current circumstances.

**Marketing Fatigue**

Repeated and irrelevant offers can reduce customer engagement and trust.

**The problem isn't a lack of banking products. It's knowing which product is right for each customer, and when.**

# SLIDE 04 — OUR SOLUTION

# Introducing Cuebik.

Cuebik is an agentic AI banking platform that analyzes customer financial behaviour, banking activities and individual interests to recommend relevant financial products and automates personalized engagement across multiple channels.

### What Cuebik Does

**Understands Customers**

Analyzes financial behaviour, transaction patterns, banking app interactions, interests and individual financial goals.

**Matches Relevant Products**

Uses an AI/ML recommendation engine to identify banking products and services suited to individual customers.

**Automates Customer Engagement**

Uses a central AI agent to coordinate personalized recommendations, communication timing and customer outreach.

**Enables Omnichannel Delivery**

Connects with customers through banking apps, email, SMS and WhatsApp.

**Empowers Banking Teams**

Provides customer insights, recommendation analytics, AI agent monitoring and operational controls through an internal dashboard.

**One Platform. Personalized Banking Recommendations. Intelligent Engagement.**

↓

### 2. AI/ML RECOMMENDATION ENGINE

### 3. CENTRAL AI AGENT
